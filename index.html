How it works

Three steps from short-staffed to sorted

  1. Step 01

    Tell us what you need

    Set the role, the site, the hours and any tickets the job requires. A single shift and a twelve-week project are posted the same way.

  2. Step 02

    Review the people matched

    We surface available workers who fit. You see verified documents, shift history and ratings from the businesses they have worked for.

  3. Step 03

    Confirm and get to work

    Pick your team and they are booked. Hours are tracked against the shift, so there is one clear record at the end of the week.

Why WorkBridge

Staffing support without the usual friction

We built WorkBridge around the two complaints we heard most: agencies that are slow to answer, and workers who never know what the job involves.

Screened, not scraped

Every worker is checked for right-to-work, relevant tickets and references before a single profile reaches you.

Fast where it counts

Most requests are matched within the hour. Urgent cover posted in the morning is usually confirmed before the afternoon.

Scales both ways

Add ten people for a peak week and drop back to two the week after, without carrying the cost in between.

Clear from the start

Rates, hours, site details and expectations are on the shift before anyone accepts it. Fewer surprises on both sides.

People who answer

A named account contact for regular clients, and support that stays reachable through the shift, not just before you book it.

One record of the week

Hours are tracked against each shift, so approvals and invoicing come from the same numbers everyone already saw.
