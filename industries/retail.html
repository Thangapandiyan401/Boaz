Retail staff for peak trade and everything around it

Sale weekends, stocktake, a store reset overnight, or simply covering leave in December. Retail demand is spiky, so your team should be able to flex.

Request staff How it works

Why this sector is different

Retail demand does not arrive evenly. Sale weekends, a store reset overnight, stocktake, and the December run all need more people than the standing roster carries.

What we verify first

  • Prior retail or customer-facing experience
  • Police check where a role handles cash or high-value stock
  • Availability confirmed for overnight and early-morning resets

Available roles

Roles we fill most often

Sales assistants

Floor staff who can greet, help and close without a week of training first.

Register and service desk

POS-experienced staff for peak trade, returns and click-and-collect.

Stockroom crew

Receiving, unpacking, ticketing and replenishment, often outside trading hours.

Visual merchandisers

Window changes, planogram resets and seasonal campaign rollouts.

Stocktake teams

Counting crews for scheduled stocktakes across single or multiple sites.

Concierge and hosts

Queue management and customer flow in centres and flagship stores.

Overnight resets and stocktakes are usually easiest to fill when posted five to seven days ahead.
