Staff your busiest weeks without hiring permanently

From a single shift to a full seasonal crew, WorkBridge gives operations teams a reliable bench of workers who are checked, rated and ready to go.

Talk to our team Browse industries

Cover the gaps you can predict, and the ones you cannot

Some staffing gaps are on the calendar months out: a trade show, a stocktake, the December run. Others land on a Tuesday morning when two people call in sick. WorkBridge is built to handle both without you keeping spare headcount on the books.

  • Post a single shift, a week of cover, or a rolling seasonal crew.
  • Re-book people who already know your site instead of starting again.
  • Set the tickets and clearances a role needs, and only see workers who hold them.
  • One record of hours worked, so approvals and invoicing agree.

What you get

Built for the person holding the roster

Requests, not job ads

Describe the shift once. No ad to write, no inbox of applications to work through, no phone screening before you know who is free.

Compliance handled first

Right-to-work, tickets and role-specific clearances are verified during onboarding, not chased on the morning of the shift.

A bench, not strangers

Rate the people who work well and ask for them again. Regular sites build up a group who already know the floor.

Where it helps most

Three situations we hear about every week

Short notice

Someone calls in sick

A morning request for same-day cover in a metro area is usually confirmed within a couple of hours, because the people who could take it are already screened and already available.

Seasonal

Volume triples for six weeks

Build a crew for the peak, keep the ones who work out, and stand the rest down when it passes. No redundancy conversation at the end of it.

One-off

A project with a hard date

A store reset, a stocktake, a two-day exhibition build. Staff a fixed piece of work without adding to the permanent headcount.
