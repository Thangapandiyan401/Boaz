Privacy policy

How we handle personal information collected through this website and our staffing services.

Last updated 1 August 2026. This document is a starting template for the WorkBridge website build and should be reviewed by a qualified legal adviser before the site goes live.

Who we are

WorkBridge Staffing Pty Ltd (“WorkBridge”, “we”, “us”) operates this website and provides flexible staffing services to businesses and work opportunities to individuals in Australia.

What we collect

We collect personal information you give us directly, which may include:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number and employer.
  • Information you include in an enquiry or message to us.
  • For people seeking work: identity and right-to-work documents, qualifications, tickets, work history, references and payment details.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device type and the pages you visit, in order to keep the site working and to understand which content is useful.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide our services.
  • To verify that a worker holds the credentials a role requires.
  • To match workers with suitable shifts, and to share the relevant parts of a worker profile with a business considering them.
  • To meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with a business beyond what that business needs to make a hiring decision.

Who we share it with

We may share personal information with service providers who help us operate (for example hosting, identity verification or payment processing), with a prospective host business where you have applied for or accepted a shift, and with regulators or law enforcement where we are legally required to.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires us to. Records relating to work performed are generally retained for seven years.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected individuals and the regulator where a data breach is likely to cause serious harm.

Your rights

You can ask for access to the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. You can also ask us to delete information we no longer need. Write to privacy@workbridge.com.au and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Our use of cookies and similar technologies is described in our cookie policy.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your personal information, contact us first. If we cannot resolve it, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed, and material changes will be highlighted on this page.
